Instructions & Consent

In this study, you will evaluate a series of short research proposals. For each proposal, please select the response that best reflects your own judgment.

There are no right or wrong answers. This is not a test, and you will not be judged in any way. Some proposals may feel clear, others ambiguous. Please rely on your immediate impression.

Your participation is voluntary and anonymous. You may stop at any time. No personal identifying information will be collected.

  • You will be shown about 50 short texts to evaluate.
  • No identifying data will be stored.
  • Approx. duration: 8-10 minutes.
